# About me

Currently I'm a post-doctorate at [CESCO](https://cesco.mnhn.fr/fr) (a laboratory of the French [National Muséum of Natural History](https://www.mnhn.fr/fr)). I work on modelling different sampling design by taking into account participatory science challenges, for the deployment of acoustic recorders within the [BIODICAPT project](https://eng-pepr-agroeconum.custom.hub.inrae.fr/funded-projects/socio-ecosystem/cfp-awarded-projects/biodicapt).

For that, I use methods of "optimal experimental designs", which is a complicated way to say that I am looking for the best way to collect a maximum of information on our species of interest, with minimal cost.
